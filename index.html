Method. Supply-side scenario, McKinsey Power of Parity logic. New workers: Δ = (1 − FLFP) × barrier × coverage × activation. Value added ≈ workers × GDP per capita (conservative: per-capita < per-worker). Wage-gap effect = existing female workers × GDP/cap × wage gap × share closed. Real 2024: female/male LFP (WB/ILO; Taiwan DGBAS), GDP per capita nominal (WB/IMF). Population & wage-gap figures are editable estimates. This is the size of the prize, not value attributable to the passport alone. Skills Pass is one enabler among several. This interactive scenario is not a causal forecast.
